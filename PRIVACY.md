# Privacy Policy — Instagram Recommendation Blocker

_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

Instagram Recommendation Blocker ("the extension") is designed to work entirely
on your own device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
or usage data.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves the on/off state of its three switches ("Block For you",
"Block Reels", "Block Explore contents") using your browser's local extension
storage (`chrome.storage` / `browser.storage`). This is used only to remember
your preferences between sessions. It never leaves your browser.

## What the extension accesses

The extension runs only on `instagram.com`. It reads and modifies the Instagram
page in your browser to hide recommendation surfaces. It does not read, record,
or transmit the content of any page, your account information, your messages, or
your browsing activity.

## Data collection and sharing

- No personal data is collected.
- No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising code is included.
- No remote code is loaded or executed.

## Permissions

- **storage** — to remember your switch preferences on your device.
- **Host access to `instagram.com`** — so the content script can hide
  recommendation surfaces on Instagram pages.
